Find your foothold in the New River Valley.

Blacksburg is a place to slow down, look around, and make the day your own. Follow a trail through the New River Valley, wander Virginia Tech’s distinctive campus, or find a new favorite around downtown’s independent shops and restaurants.

A mountain town, your kind of pace

Big views. Small-town welcome.

Come for a game, a campus visit, a weekend outdoors, or simply a change of scenery. However you get here, leave a little room for the unexpected.

A good first day

Start with coffee downtown, take a self-guided look around campus, and save the afternoon for the Huckleberry Trail or another outdoor stop. Finish with dinner, a performance, or a game if one is on the calendar. Swap the order, skip a stop, and make the plan yours.

Local tip

Check the calendar before you book

Virginia Tech events can make a visit feel electric—and change how busy town gets. Check the university events calendar and reserve lodging early for your dates.

Events

An event can turn a good visit into a memorable one. Catch the energy of a campus gathering, plan an evening around a performance, or find a local happening that gives you another reason to stay out a little longer.

Make an event part of your itinerary

  • Before you go: Confirm the date, start time, venue address, tickets, age guidance, and accessibility information with the organizer.
  • Plan the trip: Check parking, arrival instructions, and local transportation options before event day.
  • Book early: Major university events can affect lodging availability and restaurant wait times.
  • Leave a little room: Add time for traffic, finding your seat, and a meal before or after.
Schedules change

Always confirm with the organizer

Event dates, ticket availability, venues, and entry details can change. The linked calendars are the best place to check current information; verify the final details directly before traveling.

No event on your dates?

Blacksburg still makes a good day out. Explore campus, downtown, and nearby trails, find a place for dinner on the dining guide, or plan an easy-going weekend in town.

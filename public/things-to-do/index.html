Things to Do

Some of the best Blacksburg days mix a little campus, a little downtown, and time outside. Pick one anchor for the day, then leave room to follow your curiosity.

Choose your adventure

Walk, roll, wander Explore the Huckleberry Trail The paved Huckleberry Trail connects Blacksburg and Christiansburg and is a welcoming option for a walk or bike ride. Check the trail organization for current maps and conditions. Take campus at your own pace

Virginia Tech’s Blacksburg campus is open to visitors. Check the university’s visit guide for tours, campus highlights, and visitor parking information. If you are attending a game or another major event, make a parking and arrival plan before you leave.

Find a little fresh air

The Huckleberry Trail is a paved route connecting the Blacksburg and Christiansburg communities. The trail organization describes it as a 15-mile trail and part of a broader regional trail system; check its maps and trail information for current access and conditions.

For more outdoor possibilities, explore the regional guide and Montgomery County visitor information. Distances, access, and seasonal conditions vary, so confirm details before heading out.

Make room for a plan B

Pair an outdoor stop with a downtown meal, browse the events calendar, or trade a rainy afternoon outdoors for a campus performance or museum visit. Check opening hours, tickets, and accessibility details directly with each destination.

Plan with care

A trail day is better with a quick check

Before setting out, check weather, trail conditions, parking, and any posted closures. Bring water and choose a route that fits your group and the day’s conditions.

Keep exploring

Find places to eat on the dining guide, or turn your shortlist into a relaxed day or weekend itinerary.

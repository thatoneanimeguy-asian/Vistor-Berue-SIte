Blacksburg sits in the New River Valley of Southwest Virginia, where the energy of Virginia Tech meets the quieter pleasures of a mountain-town visit. A day here can be as full or unhurried as you like: walk a campus path, browse downtown, catch a performance, or head out for fresh air.

## A town shaped by place

Virginia Tech is part of the rhythm of Blacksburg. The campus draws students, families, alumni, researchers, and visitors from around the world, while downtown brings people together over food, shops, and community life. Beyond town, the landscape opens into wooded trails, ridgelines, and the wider New River Valley.

## Before you reserve

- Check the exact address and how long it takes to reach the places on your itinerary.
- Confirm parking availability, fees, and any event-day restrictions.
- Review check-in times, cancellation terms, deposits, and included amenities.
- Ask directly about accessibility features, pet policies, and room needs important to your group.
- Book early for Virginia Tech games, commencement, family weekends, and other high-demand dates.

{{< visitor-note eyebrow="Busy dates" title="Let the event calendar guide your timing" >}}
University events can affect room availability and prices. Check the [Virginia Tech calendar](https://events.vt.edu/) before choosing dates, and contact the lodging provider to confirm current rates and policies.
{{< /visitor-note >}}

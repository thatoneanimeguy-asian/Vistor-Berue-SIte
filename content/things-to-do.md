## Take campus at your own pace

Virginia Tech's Blacksburg campus is open to visitors. Check the university's [visit guide](https://www.vt.edu/visit.html) for tours, campus highlights, and [visitor parking information](https://parking.vt.edu/parking/visitors.html). If you are attending a game or another major event, make a parking and arrival plan before you leave.

## Find a little fresh air

The Huckleberry Trail is a paved route connecting the Blacksburg and Christiansburg communities. The trail organization describes it as a 15-mile trail and part of a broader regional trail system; check its [maps and trail information](https://www.huckleberrytrail.org/) for current access and conditions.

For more outdoor possibilities, explore the [regional guide](https://stepintoblacksburg.org/) and [Montgomery County visitor information](https://www.gotomontva.com/). Distances, access, and seasonal conditions vary, so confirm details before heading out.

## Make room for a plan B

Pair an outdoor stop with a downtown meal, browse the [events calendar]({{< relref "events.md" >}}), or trade a rainy afternoon outdoors for a campus performance or museum visit. Check opening hours, tickets, and accessibility details directly with each destination.

{{< visitor-note eyebrow="Plan with care" title="A trail day is better with a quick check" >}}
Before setting out, check weather, trail conditions, parking, and any posted closures. Bring water and choose a route that fits your group and the day's conditions.

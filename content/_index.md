## A good first day

Start with coffee downtown, take a self-guided look around campus, and save the afternoon for the Huckleberry Trail or another outdoor stop. Finish with dinner, a performance, or a game if one is on the calendar. Swap the order, skip a stop, and make the plan yours.

{{< visitor-note eyebrow="Local tip" title="Check the calendar before you book" >}}
Virginia Tech events can make a visit feel electric—and change how busy town gets. Check the [university events calendar](https://events.vt.edu/) and reserve lodging early for your dates.
{{< /visitor-note >}}

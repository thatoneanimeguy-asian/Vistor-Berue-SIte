## Itinerary ideas

### A day in Blacksburg

1. **Start downtown.** Grab breakfast or coffee and explore the area around Main Street.
2. **Make time for campus.** Walk through Virginia Tech and check its visitor guide for tours, highlights, and parking.
3. **Take a lunch break.** Choose a place downtown or near your next stop.
4. **Get outside.** Walk or bike a section of the Huckleberry Trail, checking current access and conditions first.
5. **Leave the evening open.** Find dinner, a performance, or a game if one is on the calendar.

### A weekend in the New River Valley

**Day one:** Get oriented downtown, explore Virginia Tech, and settle in for a local dinner. If an event is on the calendar, give yourself time for it.

**Day two:** Spend the morning on a trail or exploring the region. Return to Blacksburg for lunch and one last stop before heading home.

These are flexible ideas, not timed schedules. Check travel times, opening hours, trail conditions, and parking before you set out.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should I see if I only have a few hours?

Choose either downtown Blacksburg or Virginia Tech's campus, depending on your interests. Both can pair well with a meal; check campus visitor information before you go.

### Do I need to plan around Virginia Tech events?

It can help. Campus events may affect lodging demand, traffic, parking, and availability. Check the university's current calendar and confirm travel details before you arrive.

### Can I get around without a car?

It depends on your itinerary and where you are staying. Check current local transit options, walking routes, and service schedules before relying on them.

### Are trails open year-round?

Conditions and access can change with weather or maintenance. Check current trail information before heading out and choose footwear and clothing for the conditions.

### Where can I find current hours and prices?

Hours, prices, and reservation policies can change. Confirm details directly with each restaurant, lodging provider, attraction, or event organizer.

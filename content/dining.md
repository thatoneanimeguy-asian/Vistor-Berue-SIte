## Follow your appetite

- **Coffee and breakfast:** Start near downtown or campus, then check whether the kitchen opens early enough for your plans.
- **A quick bite:** Look for counter-service cafés and casual spots when you want to get back out exploring.
- **A longer lunch or dinner:** Browse current menus and make a reservation when available, especially on busy university weekends.
- **Something different:** Search for international cuisines, vegetarian options, bakeries, and seasonal menus to find a meal that fits your group.

## Make dining part of the route

Pair a downtown meal with a walk through local shops, grab a bite between campus activities, or plan dinner after the Huckleberry Trail. Search the map above to compare locations and routes.

{{< visitor-note eyebrow="A little planning goes a long way" title="Check today's details" >}}
Menus, hours, prices, reservations, and dietary accommodations can change. Confirm directly with the restaurant, and let them know about allergies or other needs before ordering.
